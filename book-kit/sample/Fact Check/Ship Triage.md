# Ship Triage — the one working list of what changes before the book ships

Written by the Editor. Each open finding (from `Fact Check/Findings Register.md`, `Punch List.md` and the hand-off inboxes) gets one row in one lane. The Editor cards the chapters in the order of the **Chapter order** list; the Watcher checks that order. A row leaves the list when its card is applied or the author rules it closed. Nothing is taken out of the book from this list without the author's approval.

**Lanes:**

- **MUST** — a publication risk, one card each: a cited event whose facts are wrong, or a quotation that is truncated or wrong. A card, before ship.
- **WORDING** — a prose fault under the rulebook (a banned word, an abstraction acting, a telegraph, an invented person). A card.
- **SOURCE-FIX** — the source is in hand and the citation is wrong or incomplete (locator, edition, title, the read not logged). A card on the note, after the read is logged.
- **SOURCE-CUT** — nobody can find the source. The claim stays as written, an ask goes to the author for the source, and a card proposing a cut reaches the author only if the author asks for one.
- **SOURCE-SHIP** — ships as is: the citation ships as it stands on the author's recorded exception (`decision.recorded`, and the Claims row says so).
- **DROP** — no change to the book; the finding asked for something the book does not need, for the reason given.

**Columns:** `where` (`Ch##/L##` or the note slug) · `lane` · `finding` (one plain sentence, with the file it came from) · `card` (the card's title once built) · `state` (open, carded, applied, closed — with the date).
