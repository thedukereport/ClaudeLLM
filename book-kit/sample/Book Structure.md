# Book Structure

The outline of the book: each chapter's title, its job in the whole argument, and the sections it holds. Production keeps this file. Locks are in `Fact Check/Locks.md`, never here.

**For each chapter:** the number and title as in the chapter file's name; one sentence on the chapter's job; the section headings in order; the chapters it points to and the chapters that point to it (for `tools/check-crossrefs.sh`); and a note when the chapter teaches a pattern by quoting it, so a sweep treats those quotations as mentions (Rules — Editor).
