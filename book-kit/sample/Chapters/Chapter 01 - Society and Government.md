# Chapter 1 — Society and Government<!-- b:1298a0 -->

Thomas Paine's pamphlet *Common Sense* reached its readers in 1776, and the edition printed by W. and T. Bradford in Philadelphia is dated February 14, 1776.[^paine_edition]<!-- b:8b163b -->

Paine opened his argument by pulling apart two words his readers used as one: "Society in every state is a blessing, but government even in its best state is but a necessary evil; in its worst state an intolerable one."[^paine_society] The sentence carries the whole pamphlet's frame.<!-- b:db8052 -->

He gave each word its own origin: "Society is produced by our wants, and government by our wickedness."[^paine_wants]<!-- b:73bd76 -->
