# Chapter 2 — Reflection and Choice<!-- b:b65f9d -->

Alexander Hamilton addressed the first *Federalist* paper "To the People of the State of New York," and the Project Gutenberg text heads it "For the Independent Journal."[^fed1_heading]<!-- b:0bdbc7 -->

Hamilton framed the ratification debate as a test: whether "societies of men are really capable or not of establishing good government from reflection and design, or whether they are forever destined to depend for their political constitutions on accident and force."[^fed1_test]<!-- b:3204ff -->
