How this page works

One card at a time; Previous and Next, or the arrow keys, move you through. Your desk, the bar you open and close under the buttons, lists what Claude has checked and sent back to you, the new cards, and what Claude has not yet read; its top line shows the counts, and the browser tab shows the number ready for you. Waiting for you holds what needs your decision. Approved, going in holds what you approved that is not in the chapter yet. In the book holds what is done; every button there still works. Asks holds what Claude needs from you, one card at a time. Reopen? shows, whole, a card that would change a line you approved; Reopen this line sends it to Waiting for you, and Keep it closed ends it. Back-fill lists the works the book cites that Claude cannot find on the local shelves.

What the buttons mean
Do itYes to the question as asked.
Don't do itNo. The source comes out and your sentence stays, or the card's change doesn't go in.
SkipNot now. It stays on the list.
DeleteTake out the source and the passage it supports.
Each Don't do it or Delete comes back to you as a proposal card, with the exact text, for you to approve. Where a passage has a Proposal box, you can edit the proposed paragraph and footnote directly. Claude checks each edit you send against the source and the chapter, and writes the result in the box. Once you approve, the change goes into the chapter by unlock, edit, relock, push.
