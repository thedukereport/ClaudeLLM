Source Rulings

One card at a time; Previous and Next, or the arrow keys, move you through. A green box at the top lists what Claude has checked and sent back to you, and the browser tab shows the number. Waiting for you holds what needs your decision. Approved, going in holds what you approved that is not in the chapter yet. In the book holds what is done; every button there still works. Asks holds what Claude needs from you, one card at a time. Back-fill lists the works the book cites with no logged read.

What the buttons mean
Do itYes to the question as asked.
Don't do itNo. The source comes out and your sentence stays, or the card's change doesn't go in.
SkipNot now. It stays on the list.
DeleteTake out the source and the passage it supports.
Each Don't do it or Delete comes back to you as a proposal card, with the exact text, for you to approve. Where a passage has a Proposal box, you can edit the proposed paragraph and footnote directly. Claude checks each edit you send against the source and the chapter, and writes the result in the box. Once you approve, the change goes into the chapter by unlock, edit, relock, push.
