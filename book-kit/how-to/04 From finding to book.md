# From finding to book: the life of one change

1. **Found.** The Fact-checker, the Editor, the nightly agent or the Watcher finds something: a quotation that differs from its source, a note that holds an argument, a banned word, a cited work nobody opened.
2. **Fact-checked first.** A suspected error is checked by reading the source, starting in your own library (`tools/have.sh`, `tools/find-source.sh`, `tools/find-page.sh`, the local source servers), and logged before it reaches you: a Claims row, a ledger section, a `source.read` event. A search that finds nothing is logged too, with what was searched.
3. **Recorded, not judged.** The finding says what was read, where, and what it says: "the source says X; the body says Y". It never says a claim is true or false.
4. **A card.** The Editor or QA builds it (`Methodology/How a Card Is Built.md`): the paragraph's job, the paragraph above and below, the Current text and the Proposed text in full, what changes and why, and the check against the rules. Before it goes in: `tools/card-log.py --write` for its reads, `tools/card_reads.py --card`, `tools/check-card-notes.py --card` and `tools/check-card-closed.py --card`. Then `tools/cards.py add <card.json> --why "…"` puts it in the store, and a second session audits its prose (`tools/prose.py --record-card`).
5. **Your ruling.** On the card desk, once every gate passes: Approve, edit and send back for checking, or write a directive. Your words on a card are kept as you wrote them, in the event log.
6. **Checked again, then applied.** Your Approve rules on the ideas; the card is still fact-checked and language-checked before it goes in. Production runs `tools/apply-card.py <card> --write` (dry run first), which refuses if the chapter is locked or the text on disk has moved, writes through `blockedit` so block ids survive, writes the ledger entry, marks the card applied in the store, and refreshes or sends back every other open card of the chapter. Production then commits and prints the push line for you.
7. **Settled.** The line does not come back for a better word. When a later reading shows it wrong, it comes back as a whole card in the desk's Reopen? lane.
